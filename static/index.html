    2Deep Dive: Model Architectures & Mathematical Formulation

    The prototype implements 6 distinct learning paradigms, trained from scratch with pure NumPy and Adam optimizer.

    Single-Frame Baseline 965 Params
    y = Softmax(W_2 • ReLU(W_1 • x_t + b_1) + b_2)
    Spatial Snapshot: Takes only the latest frame vector $x_t$ without temporal memory. Serves as the ablation baseline to prove that time-series sequence history improves accuracy from 64% to >75%.
    0.05 msFeedforward
    Elman RNN 3,781 Params
    h_t = tanh(W_x • x_t + W_h • h_{t-1} + b)
    Recurrent State: Propagates hidden state $h_t$ across all 6 time-steps via Backpropagation Through Time (BPTT). High parameter efficiency with 89.4% accuracy on synthetic lots.
    0.07 msBPTT Unfolded
    LSTM Network 23,429 Params
    f_t = σ(W_f • [x_t, h_{t-1}] + b_f) i_t = σ(W_i • [x_t, h_{t-1}] + b_i) c_t = f_t ⊙ c_{t-1} + i_t ⊙ tanh(...) h_t = σ(W_o • [x_t, h_{t-1}] + b_o) ⊙ tanh(c_t)
    Constant Error Carousel: Forget gate prevents catastrophic forgetting of morning rush hours. Highest full recall (0.94), ensuring lots near capacity are never missed.
    0.13 msGated Memory
    Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) 18,693 Params
    z_t = σ(W_z • x_t + U_z • h_{t-1} + b_z) r_t = σ(W_r • x_t + U_r • h_{t-1} + b_r) h_t = (1 - z_t) ⊙ n_t + z_t ⊙ h_{t-1}
    Streamlined Recurrence: Combines cell and hidden state into $h_t$ using update ($z$) and reset ($r$) gates. 20% fewer parameters than LSTM with identical test accuracy (89.2%).
    0.10 msDefault Model
    K-Means + CNN Clustered Manifold
    μ_k = argmin Σ ||Φ(x_i) - μ_k||^2
    Unsupervised Structure: Uses CNN feature representations projected into a K-means latent clustering space. Clusters reveal recurring parking density archetypes regardless of weather lighting.
    0.15 msHybrid Clustering
    GAN (D-Backbone) 64-d Latent
    D(x) → f_{conv} ∈ R^{64} → Classifier
    Adversarial Feature Learning: Uses a Generative Adversarial Network where the Discriminator's penultimate layer acts as a self-supervised visual backbone immune to camera noise artifacts.
    0.18 msSelf-Supervised

    3Simulation Engine & Noise Channels

    Top-Down 36-Slot Parking Physics

    Real parking CCTV datasets (like PIL-park or PKLot) often lack continuous 15-minute sequence intervals or are behind credentialed portals. ParkOcc-AI features a mathematical lot synthesizer:

    • Lot Geometry: 36 demarcated stalls with realistic asphalt texture, lane markings, and vehicle footprints.
    • Markov Arrival Transitions: Cars enter and vacate based on time-of-day arrival probabilities (surging at 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM).
    • Lot-Disjoint Split: 28 lots for training, 6 lots for validation, 6 held-out test lots. Zero overlap ensures generalization to unseen geometries.

    4 Simulated Noise Channels (Robustness Testing)

    • 1. Weather Lighting Jitter: Clear sunlight with harsh shadows, overcast diffused light, and rain reflectivity.
    • 2. Transient Spikes (2%): Temporary passing vehicles or birds blocking stalls.
    • 3. Unusable Frames (1%): Heavy camera glare, lens droplets, or darkness flagged via Laplacian blur and contrast filtering.
    • 4. Structural Slot Bias: Individual lots exhibit preference for front stalls over rear stalls.

    4Safety Guarantees & Confusion Dynamics

    Zero Extreme Errors (Empty ↔ Full Safety)

    In safety-critical parking guidance, confusing a Full lot for Empty results in driver gridlock and frustration. Throughout test lot evaluations, ParkOcc-AI recorded 0 Empty ↔ Full confusions across all models. All classification errors are constrained to adjacent ordinal bands (e.g., Low mistaken for Medium).

    Underestimation Bias Protection

    During heavy rain or glare, optical contrast drops, which occasionally causes dark cars to merge with wet asphalt. Consequently, models have a slight underestimation bias. The system incorporates an Asymmetric Safety Advice Rule: whenever a lot is classified as High, drivers are advised that it may be practically Full, preventing unexpected lot lockouts.
